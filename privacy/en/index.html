1Introduction & scope

This policy describes how the OurPins team ("we", "us") handles personal and other information in the shared-map app "OurPins" (the App) and its related website. By using the App, you agree to this policy.

2Information we collect

We collect the following information to the extent necessary to provide the service.

(1) Account information

  • Email address and password. These are securely managed by our authentication provider, Supabase Auth; passwords are stored hashed and we never hold them in plain text. When you sign up or reset your password, we send a verification code to your email.
  • When you sign in with Apple or Google, we use the provider’s identifier and any email address or name it supplies to create and authenticate your account.
  • Display name, a unique username (@handle), a short bio (optional), an avatar (initials/emoji, or an optional uploaded profile photo), and a theme color.

(2) Information you create or post in the App

  • Personal and group maps, membership, invite codes and lists.
  • Place pins, source links, addresses, coordinates, photos, notes and who wants to go or has visited.
  • Visit photos, dates, star ratings and notes, along with likes and comments on posts.
  • Friend requests, accepted friendships and blocks. When someone reports content, we retain the reporter, reason, additional details and a snapshot of the content for moderation.
  • When you import a social post, we process the link, text or image you submit. To find candidate places, we may retrieve the original post and send its link, caption and any attached screenshot to OpenAI.

(3) Location information

  • With your location permission, we use your current location for map display and nearby place searches.
  • Sharing your location with friends is off by default. Only after you choose specific friends and start sharing, we temporarily store your latest coordinates, accuracy and update time on Supabase while you use the App, and show your location to those selected friends. We do not automatically share it with every friend or group member.
  • We do not create a movement history. Locations without an update for two minutes are excluded from display, and expired positions are deleted periodically. Stopping sharing deletes the stored current position. If the device is offline, the stop request is retried after connectivity returns; positions that stop updating become invisible when they expire.
  • Location data (latitude/longitude, etc.) that you attach to a place pin is stored so it can be shared within your group.

(4) Device & technical information

  • A push-notification token (an identifier issued by Expo's notification service) and platform type (iOS / Android, etc.).
  • In-app settings such as notification preferences and the read state of pins.

(5) Support requests

For in-app support requests, we use your message, account ID and registered email address. We store the request for follow-up and may notify the support team through our email provider.

(6) Usage & diagnostic information (analytics and crash reports)

To improve the quality of the App, we analyze usage and detect bugs and crashes.

  • When usage reporting is enabled under Settings → Privacy, we record eligible event names, your account ID and timestamps in Supabase. You can switch it off to stop future reporting. These events exclude post text, photos and precise location.
  • When crash reporting is active, Sentry receives diagnostic information such as errors, app and device details, and the actions leading up to an error.

Advertising and purchases

We request non-personalized ads through Google AdMob. On iOS, the advertising SDK starts only after tracking permission is granted. It may process device identifiers, IP addresses, ad impressions, interactions and diagnostics for ad delivery, measurement and fraud prevention. Where available, ad consent options are in Settings → Privacy.

Where in-app purchases are available, RevenueCat processes an app user identifier, store purchase history and entitlement status to verify and restore purchases.

(7) Device permissions

The App uses the following permissions for their stated purposes, with your consent. You can change permissions at any time in your device settings.

PermissionPurpose

3How we use information

We use the information we collect for the following purposes.

  • To provide the App, authenticate users, and manage accounts
  • To share and sync pins, lists and visits in real time within your group
  • To deliver push and in-app notifications
  • To investigate and fix bugs and to respond to inquiries and feedback
  • To improve the App and consider new features through usage analysis and crash/bug detection
  • To respond to violations, prevent misuse, and otherwise operate the service safely

4Sharing within groups

Your personal map is for you. Pins, visit posts, photos, comments and reactions on a group map are shown to that group’s members. Live location is shared separately with the friends you choose.

Members may retain copies of photos or photo links, including after a block. Anyone with an existing photo URL may be able to open the image. Check photos before sharing them.

6Storage & deletion

App data is stored on servers managed by our third-party provider Supabase. Data may be stored on servers outside your country.

  • We retain information for as long as necessary to achieve the purposes above, or for the period required by law.
  • You can delete content you created (pins, comments, photos, etc.) using features within the App.
  • You can delete your account at any time from within the App. When you do, your account information and posted content are deleted as core data. See the account deletion guide for steps.

However, we may retain some data for a certain period to comply with the law, prevent misuse, and respond to security issues. Some content already shared to a group may also remain in other members' environments.

7Security

We take appropriate technical and organizational measures to prevent leakage, loss, or damage of the information we hold, and to otherwise manage it safely. Communications are encrypted, and database access is restricted to group members through Row Level Security (RLS). However, we cannot guarantee perfect security for communications or storage over the internet.

8Children

The App is not primarily intended for use by children. If we learn that we have collected a child's personal information without parental consent, please contact us; we will verify and promptly delete it.

9Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as needed. For significant changes, we will notify you on this page or within the App. If you continue to use the App after a change, you are deemed to have agreed to the updated policy.

10Contact

For questions about this policy, or requests to disclose, correct, or delete your information, please contact our privacy desk at ourpins.pr@gmail.com.

Day Maxer Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 19, 2026

Day Maxer helps you plan and review your day. This policy describes the data used by the iOS app. Contact legal@ourpins.app with privacy questions.

Schedules and settings

Plans, actual activity records, categories, and app settings are stored in a local SQLite database on your device. Widgets can read a limited copy through the app's local shared container. Day Maxer does not sync this schedule database to its server. Device backup is controlled by your iOS settings.

Account

When you use AI input, you sign in with Apple. Your Apple identity token, an account identifier, and any email address Apple shares are processed by Supabase Auth to create and maintain your account. Day Maxer stores your session securely on your device. You can use local planning features without signing in.

AI input

When you submit text or a voice recording for AI planning, the submitted content and necessary context, such as the current date, time zone, locale, and available category names, are sent to the Day Maxer Supabase Edge Function and to Google's Gemini API to transcribe and suggest a plan. Review and explicitly add a suggestion before it is saved locally. Day Maxer does not store the text, audio, transcript, or generated plan on its server. Google processes these requests under its applicable Gemini API terms and privacy notice.

For safety limits and diagnostics, the server stores your account ID, request time, model, result status, latency, retry status, and token counts for up to 30 days. It does not store your prompt or audio in this usage table.

Notifications and ads

Schedule notifications are generated on your device. The current public app does not display third-party ads or use an advertising SDK. A future version may introduce ads with an updated policy and disclosures.

Sharing and retention

Day Maxer does not share your locally stored plans with other users. Supabase provides account and server functions; Google processes AI requests. These providers may process data outside your country. Account and usage records are retained only as described above or as required for security and legal obligations.

Your choices and deletion

You can delete plans and activity records in the app. In Settings, you can delete your account; this removes the Supabase account and revokes the associated Apple authorization. Local plans and activity records remain on your device after account deletion so you can continue using them. Removing the app deletes its local app data according to iOS behavior. For assistance, contact support@ourpins.app.

Changes

We may update this policy as Day Maxer changes. The current version will be posted here.
